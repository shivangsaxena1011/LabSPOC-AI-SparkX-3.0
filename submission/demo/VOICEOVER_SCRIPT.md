# LabSPOC AI — Spoken Voiceover Script (5 Minutes 45 Seconds)

**Competition:** Galgotias University — SPARKX 3.0 International, 30-Day Innovation Challenge  
**Track:** Smart Campus & Digital Twin — AI-Powered Intelligent Lab SPOC  
**Project:** LabSPOC AI — Spatially Grounded Virtual Lab Ambassador  
**Speaker:** Shivang Saxena  

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] Opening & Motivation
> "Hello esteemed jurors and organizers of the Galgotias University SPARKX 3.0 International Innovation Challenge. My name is Shivang Saxena, representing the Oriental Institute of Science and Technology, Bhopal. Today, I am proud to present LabSPOC AI — a Spatially Grounded Virtual Lab Ambassador designed specifically for our institution's flagship Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Laboratory.
> Modern university research centers house high-value computational infrastructure, but visitors, prospective scholars, and accreditation reviewers rarely have an intelligent, spatially grounded guide to help them understand and explore these capabilities. LabSPOC AI bridges this gap."

### [0:25 – 1:05] Visitor Onboarding & Privacy
> "Let us begin on the Visitor Experience portal. When a guest arrives, they are greeted by an intuitive onboarding dialog. Instead of delivering a generic tour, LabSPOC AI customizes the journey based on the visitor's specific background. 
> Here, I can select whether I am an undergraduate student, an academic visitor, an industry executive, or an accreditation reviewer. Let's select 'Research Scientist' and choose a focused 15-minute time budget. Notice our explicit Analytics Consent notice: in compliance with Indian Digital Personal Data Protection principles, visitors have complete control over whether their session interactions are recorded. We opt-in and click 'Begin Experience'."

### [1:05 – 1:45] 3D Digital Twin Navigation
> "Here is our interactive 3D laboratory digital twin, built with Three.js and React Three Fiber. Visitors can intuitively orbit, pan, and zoom across distinct laboratory zones — including the High-Performance Server Rack Bay, the Edge AI Workbench, and the VR Calibration Bay.
> Notice our transparent engineering badge at the top: 'Illustrative Layout'. We explicitly declare that this room geometry is modeled to illustrative scale pending a future physical photogrammetric scan. 
> When I click on an equipment beacon — such as our NVIDIA DGX Station A100 — the camera smoothly focuses, and the equipment inspection card surfaces verified specifications directly from our relational database."

### [1:45 – 2:35] Conversational Assistant & Multilingual Voice
> "On the right, we have our Gemini-powered conversational assistant. Visitors can interact via text or native browser voice. Let's test the voice interface:
> *'What are the power requirements and cooling guidelines for the DGX Station?'*
> Behind the scenes, the query is embedded using Google's 3072-dimensional Gemini embeddings and matched against our approved standard operating procedures using hybrid semantic and lexical retrieval.
> Furthermore, LabSPOC AI natively supports multilingual interaction. If I ask in Hindi:
> *'प्रयोगशाला में हाई परफॉरमेंस कम्प्यूट क्लस्टर के लिए क्या नियम हैं?'*
> The assistant responds accurately in fluent Hindi with verified guidelines."

### [2:35 – 3:20] Evidence Gate & Honest Abstention
> "Notice the interactive citation brackets — [1] and [2] — embedded in the answer. Every factual claim is verifiably grounded in an approved document.
> But what happens if a visitor asks a question outside the laboratory's records? Let's test our signature innovation: the Evidence Gate.
> *'Can you give me the cafeteria daily lunch menu and the serial number of the Quantum Computer in Room 302?'*
> Notice the result: the Evidence Gate immediately detects insufficient evidence and abstains honestly: *'The requested information is not verified in approved laboratory documentation.'* LabSPOC AI never hallucinates specifications or invents institutional facts."

### [3:20 – 4:00] AI Project Demonstrator
> "Next, let's explore Module 4: the AI Project Demonstrator. Student and faculty researchers can submit their capstone projects through a public submission portal. 
> Once approved by administrators, projects are showcased here with problem statements, methodology, technology stack, and live demonstration links — such as our Edge AI Crop Disease Detector running on the Jetson AGX Orin, and our 3D Brain MRI Segmentation pipeline running on the DGX Station. These projects are dynamically linked to the equipment assets they utilize in our evidence graph."

### [4:00 – 4:40] Agenda-to-Tour Compiler
> "Now, let's look at Module 5 and our second signature innovation: the Agenda-to-Tour Compiler. With a single click on 'Compile Tour', our optimization engine generates a sequenced itinerary that respects the visitor's 15-minute time budget and persona interests.
> As I click 'Next Stop', the 3D camera glides to each verified equipment station, budgeting transit times and presenting curated summaries of verified equipment and approved research capstones."

### [4:40 – 5:15] Operations & Administration Workspace
> "Switching to the Administrator Workspace at the root domain, authorized lab coordinators have full operational control. Administrators can edit 3D zone coordinates in real time, review uploaded PDF and DOCX manuals, approve documents into the active vector index, inspect our Spatially Anchored Evidence Graph, and review consent-aware analytics and clustered frequently asked questions."

### [5:15 – 5:45] Conclusion & Acknowledgments
> "To conclude, LabSPOC AI transforms static university laboratories into dynamic, verifiable, and intelligent digital twin experiences. By anchoring conversational AI to spatial zones, enforcing strict Evidence Gate verification, and compiling personalized itineraries, we deliver a truly institutional-grade platform.
> Thank you to Galgotias University and the SPARKX 3.0 jury for this incredible challenge. I look forward to your questions during the technical defense."
